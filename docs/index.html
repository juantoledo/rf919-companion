    The radio's keys

    ---.---
    Show

    Hold a key about 0.6 s for its long press (seek, band list, lock…).

    Typed in the unit the band shows (MHz on SW and FM).

    Tool

    A frequency the radio stays on for 30 seconds is logged, with who was on air and the best RSSI and SNR. Times in UTC.

    RF919 Companion is unofficial; not affiliated with or endorsed by Raddy. Station data from radiomap.cl, shortwave schedules from EiBi, flags from flag-icons.

    Transmitters in

    Time

    For a broadcast aimed at you from a typical transmitter in each region. Solar data and forecasts from NOAA SWPC. An estimate from a simple model, which doesn't know the transmitter's power, your antenna or the local noise.

    This browser can't reach the radio over Bluetooth. Use Chrome on Android, or the Bluefy browser on iPhone, over https.

    Meanwhile, No radio runs a virtual RF919: every key, Memories and the Log work. Explore looks up any frequency either way.

    Meter bands

    Shows what the radio reports when you press one of its own buttons. The RF919 doesn't send key codes, only what changed (a frequency, channel mode, a setting…), so each press shows a guess at the button, what changed, and the raw messages. Nothing is sent to the radio.

    Off

      Runs the store sequence with fixed pauses and marks each step in the log, to find out which keys and replies the radio really uses (memory.md §8).

      Test band wipe holds SCAN on the band above (deleting all its memories), listens 5 s, then recalls CH 1.

      Type a command byte and data bytes in hex.

      Sent on FF13, like every other command; the checksum is added. Replies show in the log. An unknown command could change a setting.
